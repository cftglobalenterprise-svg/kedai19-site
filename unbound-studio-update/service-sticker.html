Frequently asked

What's the difference between die-cut and kiss-cut?+

Die-cut is trimmed exactly to your artwork's outline. Kiss-cut leaves a small backing sheet around each sticker, which is easier to peel off a shared roll.

Do the stickers survive water and sun?+

Yes — we print on waterproof matte vinyl, suitable for bottles, laptops, and outdoor use.
