Frequently asked

Can I order without visiting the shop?+

Yes — upload your file on the site or send it via WhatsApp, pick your spec, and pay online. We'll notify you when it's ready to collect.

Do you deliver 3D prints and stickers?+

Self-collection at Brinchang is default; delivery within Cameron Highlands can be arranged on request.

What file formats do you accept?+

PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC/DOCX for printing; STL or OBJ for 3D printing; AI or high-res PNG for stickers.
